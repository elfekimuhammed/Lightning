01 · Foundation

Six rules behind every screen

People feel lost when the same number has three names, when a page mixes today's position with this month's activity, and when every card looks equally important. The first two rules decide how every section looks; the rest keep the numbers straight.

Lead on the left

The most important card of every section sits on the left, on the soft Meadow gradient.

Support on the right

A white card beside it explains or extends the lead: a list, bars or a breakdown.

Position, then activity

What you have on a date comes before what changed in a period. The period control sits only above what it changes.

One name per number

A figure has one name and one home page. Other pages show it once and link there.

"Free cash" — never also "Available cash"

Same control, same size

Everything you press or type into is 48px tall. Chips and card actions are 36px. Nothing else.

Honest numbers

Estimates say "estimate". Nothing is annualised from under a year or compared with an empty period.

Numbers

  • Two decimals and thousands separators: 12,085.00
  • True minus with no space: −5,000.00 — never "– 5,000.00" or "-455.00"
  • + only for money in and gains; balances carry no sign
  • EGP after the number, smaller. A card where every value is EGP drops it from rows
  • Units: 1 piece, 60 shares, 12.500 g

Dates and casing

  • Dates 2026-09-30, months 2026-09, ranges "2026-09-01 to 2026-09-30"
  • Never "30 Sep 2026", "Mar 2026–Aug 2026" or "31/1"
  • Sentence case everywhere, including the sidebar: "Cash and bank", not "CASH & BANK"
  • Account codes follow the name, small and quiet: CIB Current CIB-CUR-EGP

Inside a lead card

Always in this order

  1. Title and meaning. The name of the number, then one line saying what it counts.
  2. One action, top right. A quiet button to the page that owns the number.
  3. The number. 48px, below a hairline, EGP after it. Always ink — a gain or a saving shows its + sign, not a colour.
  4. Its parts. Two to four rows, meters or toggle rows that open to show what is inside.
  5. One note, if it asks you to act. Otherwise nothing.

Inside a support card

The detail behind the lead

  • A list of rows, bars, toggle groups, a breakdown with its result band, or "Needs you".
  • The same header as the lead: title, meaning line, one quiet action.
  • Six rows at most, then "See all", so the pair stays level.
  • A section with no single answer — every holding, say — is one support card at full width.

The parts of a section

Numbered as in the example above

  1. Title. A short noun phrase in sentence case, 24px Bricolage: "Your position", "Cash flow". Not "Your Position" or "Cash Flow Analysis".
  2. Date on the right. "As of 2026-09-30" for what you have. For what changed, the range moves under the title and the period control takes the right.
  3. One hairline under the line, 12px below the text, 16px above the cards.
  4. One pair. Lead on the left, support on the right, 6 + 6 columns, 16px apart, aligned to the top. Or one support card at full width.
  5. 48px of canvas before the next section. No divider, no background band.

Buttons, chips and choices

Rules for sections

  • Every section opens with one line: title on the left, date or range on the right. Its cards share that date.
  • Then one pair: lead on the left, support on the right.
  • Tabs switch views of one subject. They never jump to another page.
  • A figure lives on one page. Elsewhere it appears once and links home.

12 · Rules

Make it hold together

Spacing, shape and layout values, the accessibility floor, and the list a screen is checked against before it ships.

Shape and layout

Four radii · one grid

12 · rows, menus
16 · fields
22 · cards
Pill · press
  • Content up to 1,120px beside a 272px sidebar; 12 columns, 16px gutters.
  • Every section is a pair: 6 + 6 columns, lead on the left.
  • Phones: one column with the lead first, 16px sides, 40px lead number, drawer full width.

Accessibility

  • Text 4.5:1 on its ground in light and dark; borders, icons and the focus ring 3:1.
  • Colour is never the only signal: amounts carry signs, states carry words.
  • Targets 44px or more. Esc closes the drawer and menus. Reduced motion turns transitions off.
  • Arabic text in any field gets dir="auto".

Icons and logo

  • Lucide, 1.75 stroke, 20px, current colour. No emoji, no icon font.
  • Pages: house, pie chart, chart line, wallet, settings. Rows: arrows for out, in and transfer; gem for gold; user for held for others.
  • The bolt sits beside the wordmark at 26px. Never recolour it by hand.

Before a screen ships, it has none of these

  • All-caps labels, including the sidebar
  • A vivid gradient card, or two leads in one section
  • More than five table columns, or an empty table with headers
  • Strong rose or green text for everyday amounts; grey spending bars
  • The lead on the right, or stretched to its neighbour's height
  • White or tinted panels inside a lead card
  • Text links with arrows ("Review →")
  • Dropdowns for your own data
  • Grey, blue or rose text on the lead card; ▼, ^ or − as open/close markers
  • Dates like "30 Sep 2026" or "31/1"
  • "– 5,000.00" or "-455.00" instead of −5,000.00
  • A period control above figures it doesn't change
  • The same number under two names
