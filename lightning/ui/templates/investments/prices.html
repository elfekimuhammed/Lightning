Update prices

Lightning asks the sources itself for the investments you hold: the latest close, and every month-end close it lacks. It does this by itself the first time you open it after a month ends, because month-end prices make your monthly returns. Sources: TradingView and Yahoo Finance for stocks, Mubasher for funds, and for gold an estimate from the world price and the dollar’s rate (Yahoo Finance).
